## How to read this

- `slot.missing` / `dst.*`: the written wall clock and the fired instants disagree. Evidence always names the reading and the instant.
- `cadence.*`: intervals computed from the expanded timeline, not from the expression.
- Sampled jobs are only checked where offset changes happen plus one baseline day; the reason is printed above each such job.
